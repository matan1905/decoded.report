Why This Exists

A quarterly report is not written for a reader in a hurry. The line that matters, a going concern paragraph or a fresh at-the-market program, sits somewhere between forty pages of footnotes and an exhibit nobody opens. Most people never see it until after the stock has already moved.

decoded.report reads the filings and puts the parts that change a holder's situation at the top, in order, with a link back to the document behind each one. It does not tell you what to do. It answers the questions that come before a decision: is this company printing shares, how many months of cash does it have, has it been served a delisting notice, did the auditors sign off clean. Those are all facts in the record, and the record is public.

The scores are a screening heuristic built from fixed rules, described in full on the methodology page. It is a starting point for your own reading, not a replacement for it.
